Privacy Policy

Rahal Music Bot · last updated 28 November 2026

Rahal Music Bot is a personal-use tool owned and operated by Rahal. Contact: ira9al@gmail.com.

What it does

The bot generates music and uploads Rahal's own music videos to Rahal's own YouTube channel, using Rahal's own authorization. It does not operate on anyone else's account.

What it stores

When someone opens the Telegram bot, the bot records basic profile details: Telegram id, username, name, language setting, a phone number only if the person chooses to share it with Telegram's share-contact button (Telegram confirms the number belongs to that account), the time they first joined and last used it. For the bot owner it also records usage data: the tracks made (title, style, settings) and which are starred. Where an app sign-in is offered, the id, name and email from the sign-in are recorded the same way. This is shown to the person in the bot before or at their first message. Nothing else is collected: no passwords, no tokens, no audio files, no tracking of visitors to this page.

The records are kept in a private repository that only the owner can open. They are used to run the studio and to know who uses it. To have your record deleted, email the address below and it will be removed.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with third parties. No data is sold, rented or transferred. The private repository is hosted by GitHub, which acts as the storage provider.

Authorization

Access to YouTube is granted by the channel owner through Google's own consent screen and can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google account permissions page. Revoking it stops all uploads.

Cookies and tracking

This page sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts or trackers.
